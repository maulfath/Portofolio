About Me

Hello! I am a Mathematics graduate from Universitas Diponegoro with a GPA (3.57/4.00), focusing on data analytics, data science, and business analysis—specializing in turning raw data and metrics into actionable insights to guide strategic decisions.

My practical experience was built through an independent study as a Junior Data Scientist at PT Vinix Seven Aurum and an internship at BPS-Statistics Indonesia. Through these roles, I have managed the complete data lifecycle: from extraction and cleaning to operational trend analysis, reporting, and building communicative visual dashboards for stakeholders.

To me, great analysis is not just about delivering attractive visualizations, but ensuring the team clearly understands what actionable steps to take next. That principle remains my core commitment across every data initiative.
